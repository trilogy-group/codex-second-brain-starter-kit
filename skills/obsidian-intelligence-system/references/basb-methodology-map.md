# Product BASB Methodology Map

This starter kit uses Tiago Forte's Building a Second Brain method as a product and engineering intelligence workflow. The goal is not only to keep notes; it is to move product evidence into shippable work.

## CODE

- Capture: preserve source material, repo snapshots, linked-page evidence, meeting notes, support exports, and blocked-access records before summarizing them.
- Organize: place captured material inside the product manifest boundary and route it into PARA-compatible vault zones.
- Distill: turn raw evidence into progressive summaries, capability maps, intermediate packets, and implementation anchors.
- Express: convert high-value intelligence into PRDs, specs, tickets, pull requests, runbooks, decisions, launch notes, and post-launch learnings.

## PARA

- Projects: active product bets and delivery work live in `30 Initiatives/`.
- Areas: durable product surfaces, capabilities, metrics, and owned operating domains live in `10 Areas/`.
- Resources: research, support, wiki, repo, code-reference, and intermediate-packet notes live in `40 Research/`.
- Archives: completed initiatives, retired decisions, stale source snapshots, and reusable closeout records live in `90 Archive/`.

## Progressive Summarization

Generated notes should keep raw evidence available while making the future reader faster:

- `distillation_level: raw` keeps source material intact.
- `distillation_level: highlighted` identifies important sections or evidence.
- `distillation_level: distilled` states the essence and why it matters.
- `distillation_level: executive` points to a recommended action or output.

Every durable product note should make clear what stage it is in, where it fits in PARA, how actionable it is, and whether it feeds a shippable output.

## Intermediate Packets

Intermediate packets are reusable building blocks for future work. In this repo they are usually capability briefs, code investigations, support-to-code traces, bug hypotheses, research syntheses, or launch/runbook fragments.

An intermediate packet should link back to raw evidence and forward to at least one possible output, initiative, decision, or runbook.

## Express To Shippable Work

The output of a great product second brain is not a larger vault. It is better product work. The vault should make it easy to produce:

- PRDs and product specs
- engineering tickets and implementation plans
- pull request descriptions and review checklists
- support and incident runbooks
- architecture or migration decisions
- launch notes and post-launch learning records

Delivery systems such as GitHub, Jira, Linear, or support tools remain the systems of record. Obsidian stores the reasoning, evidence, traceability, and reusable knowledge behind the work.
